About us

Twenty-five years of building across Odisha.

Babita Construction works across three connected lines — construction, mining and transportation. Owning our own plant and fleet means fewer subcontractors between you and the work, and schedules we can actually hold to.

From residential builds in Bhubaneswar to contract extraction and bulk haulage, the same engineering team and the same quality checks apply.

Design tokens

Every value below maps to a CSS custom property at the top of this file, and will map to a Tailwind token in the Next.js build. Change it once, it changes everywhere.

Two colours, clear roles. Navy #01245B carries structure — header, footer, hero, dark sections, all headings. Orange #FF5E14 is the signal — it marks the one thing you should act on in any given view. Used sparingly it stays loud; used everywhere it stops meaning anything.
Navy#01245B · primary
Navy deep#011A44 · topbar, footer
Navy soft#0A3272 · cards on dark
Orange#FF5E14 · secondary
Orange dark#E64F0A · hover
Orange ink#C43C08 · small text
Orange wash#FFF0E8 · tint
Slate#47536B · body copy
Light#F1F4F8 · alt section
Line#DDE3EC · borders
Why there are two oranges. #FF5E14 on white measures 3.06:1 — below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA floor for small text. So it is used only for fills, bars, and text sitting on navy. Any small orange text on a light background uses #C43C08 instead, at 5.25:1. Measured pairs:
white on navy  ·  14.95:1   pass AAA
navy on orange  ·  4.89:1   pass AA — buttons
orange on navy  ·  4.89:1   pass AA — eyebrows on dark
slate on white  ·  7.73:1   pass AAA — body
orange ink on white  ·  5.25:1   pass AA — eyebrows on light
